# Literacy: information literacy

> The ability to find, evaluate, and use information well.

## 1. Definition
The abilities involved in finding information, judging its credibility and relevance, and using it to answer questions or make decisions [acrl2015].

## 2. Boundaries
Narrower than general literacy; overlaps with judging [trustworthiness](trustworthiness.md) of sources, which is here a skill of the person rather than a property of the information.
