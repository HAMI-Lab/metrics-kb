# Literacy: AI literacy

> The ability to understand, use, and critically evaluate AI systems.

## 1. Definition
A set of competencies that let a person understand what AI systems do, use them effectively, and evaluate them critically [long2020].

## 2. Boundaries
About AI in general, not trust in, or skill with, one particular system.
