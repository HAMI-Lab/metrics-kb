# User experience: emotional response

> The feelings a person has while or after using or viewing something.

## 1. Definition
The emotional state a stimulus or interaction produces, often described by pleasure, arousal and dominance [bradley1994].

## 2. Boundaries
Emotion is momentary; attitudes such as [trust in the system](trust-in-system.md) last longer.
