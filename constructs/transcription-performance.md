# Transcription performance

> How well a component turns speech or images of text into the text they contain.

## 1. Definition
Output type (D23): the input is speech or an image of text, and the output is text with one correct form per item, the transcript [jurafsky2025]. Unlike short closed answers, the output is long, so errors are counted per word or character.

## 2. Boundaries
Not [closed-answer performance](closed-answer-performance.md), where the answer is short and checked whole. Not [content adequacy](content-adequacy.md), where many outputs can be good.
