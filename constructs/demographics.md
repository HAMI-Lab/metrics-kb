# Background: demographics

> Who a person is in terms of attributes such as age, gender and language.

## 1. Definition
Attributes such as age, gender, first language and country, as the person reports them.

## 2. Boundaries
Not a capability: demographics describe a person, not what the person can do. Comparing a system's outputs across groups of people is [fairness](fairness.md), a system characteristic.
