# Reliance on AI

> Whether people follow a system's advice when they decide.

## 1. Definition
A behaviour: how often and when a person adopts a system's recommendation in their decisions [lee2004; schemmer2023].

## 2. Boundaries
Differs from [trust in the system](trust-in-system.md), an attitude. Reliance is not good in itself: following wrong advice is a failure, which is what [appropriateness](appropriate-reliance.md) captures.
