# User experience: visualization aesthetics

> How visually pleasing a person finds a visualization.

## 1. Definition
The aesthetic pleasure a person feels when looking at a visual representation [he2023].

## 2. Boundaries
Distinct from readability (whether a person can read it) and from correctness of what it shows; pretty charts can mislead.
