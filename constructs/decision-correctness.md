# Task effectiveness: decision correctness

> Whether the decisions people make with the system's help are correct.

## 1. Definition
The correctness of decisions made by a person who uses a system's advice or outputs, judged against the right answer [bansal2021].

## 2. Boundaries
It judges the person and system together. The system's own accuracy on the same cases is a component property; the team can be better or worse than either alone [bansal2021].
