# Retrieval effectiveness: relevance

> How well a retrieved item fits the user's information need, in the many senses that "fit" can take.

## 1. Definition
The relation between an information item and a user's need. Information science treats relevance as having several manifestations, for example system, topical, cognitive, situational, and motivational relevance [saracevic2007].

## 2. Boundaries
Topicality is only one aspect. Users also judge items by criteria such as currency and the quality of the source [barry1998].

## 3. Narrower constructs
The narrower constructs here are aspects users judge separately. They can disagree: a topically perfect item can be outdated or untrustworthy.
