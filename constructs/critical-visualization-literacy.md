# Literacy: critical visualization literacy

> Whether a person can recognize when a visualization misleads.

## 1. Definition
The ability to read visualizations critically: to notice when a chart's design or data handling invites a wrong conclusion [ge2023].

## 2. Boundaries
Narrower than [visualization literacy](visualization-literacy.md), which also covers reading well-made charts.
