# User background

> Attributes of a person that are recorded rather than tested, such as age or education.

## 1. Definition
Facts about a person that a study records by asking, rather than measuring with a test: demographic attributes and education so far. Prior experience with similar systems is a candidate.

## 2. Boundaries
Unlike [literacy](literacy.md), background is not an ability shown by right answers; it is what the person reports about themselves. It is used to describe a sample and to explain differences in outcomes (D24).

## 3. Narrower constructs
Demographics and education answer different questions: who the participants are, and what schooling they have had.
