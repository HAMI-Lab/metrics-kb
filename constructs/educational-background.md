# Background: education

> How much formal education a person has completed, and in what field.

## 1. Definition
The highest level of formal education a person has completed, and optionally their field of study, as the person reports them.

## 2. Boundaries
Not the same as [literacy](literacy.md) or [numeracy](numeracy.md): education is a record of schooling, while literacies are abilities shown on tests. One does not stand in for the other.
