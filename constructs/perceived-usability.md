# Usability: perceived usability

> How usable people who used the system judge it to be.

## 1. Definition
Users' own judgment of how usable a system is, after using it. It corresponds roughly to the satisfaction part of the ISO definition [iso9241-11].

## 2. Boundaries
It is a judgment, not an observation of performance: people can rate a system highly and still fail tasks. It differs from [mental workload](mental-workload.md), which is about the demand of the task on the person rather than the qualities of the system.
