# Literacy: visualization literacy

> The ability to read and interpret charts and other data visualizations.

## 1. Definition
The ability to extract and interpret information from visualizations, such as reading values, comparing, and spotting trends [boy2014].

## 2. Boundaries
About reading visualizations in general, not the usability of a particular visualization tool.
