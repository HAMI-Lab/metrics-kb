# Literacy

> A person's ability to find, read, understand, and use a kind of information or technology.

## 1. Definition
The knowledge and skills that let a person understand and work with a kind of representation or technology: information, visualizations, data, AI systems.

## 2. Boundaries
Literacy belongs to the person and holds across systems. How well the same person does with a particular system is a [task characteristic](task-characteristics.md).

## 3. Narrower constructs
Each kind of literacy has its own definition and tests. They overlap (reading a chart in an AI system's answer draws on both), so a person can be strong in one and weak in another.
