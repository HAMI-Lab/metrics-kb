# Trustworthiness

> Whether a retrieved item or system output deserves to be relied on.

## 1. Definition
The degree to which information or an output can be relied on: accurate, from a credible source, and honest about its limits.

## 2. Boundaries
Trustworthiness is a property of the information or system. Trust is the user's attitude toward it; good design aims for trust that matches trustworthiness (appropriate reliance) [lee2004]. Source quality is one of the criteria users apply when judging relevance [barry1998].
