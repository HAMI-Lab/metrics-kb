# Explanation understanding

> Whether people understand how the system behaves after seeing its explanations.

## 1. Definition
The accuracy of a person's understanding of a model, shown for example by whether they can predict what it will output [doshivelez2017; hase2020].

## 2. Boundaries
Differs from [explanation faithfulness](explanation-faithfulness.md), which is judged without people. Self-reported understanding and satisfaction with explanations are related but subjective [kim2024].
